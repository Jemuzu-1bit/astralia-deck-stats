# Animal cursor assets

The cat and dog cursor animations are derived from LuizMelo's **Pet Cats Pack** and
**Pet Dogs Pack**, both released under CC0 1.0. The packed atlases used here were
redistributed by the TODAK public project with provenance and SHA-256 records.

- Cat source: https://luizmelo.itch.io/pet-cat-pack
- Dog source: https://luizmelo.itch.io/pet-dogs-pack
- Packed atlases and provenance: https://github.com/euuuuuuan/todak-public
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The generated GIF files crop, scale, and regroup selected frames for cursor states.
